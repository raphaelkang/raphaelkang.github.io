The ERA

The retirement income I help you build on top of CPF LIFE.

CPF LIFE pays you a monthly income for as long as you live, and that is a strong foundation. But it is a base, not the whole retirement. The ERA, your Enhanced Retirement Account, is the income layer I set up above it.

Built on the foundation of CPF LIFE

CPF LIFE stays at the base for life. The ERA is layered on top, so your total monthly income rises.

Income before age 65

The ERA can potentially give you a source of income before CPF LIFE starts, covering the years after you stop working.

Not a drawdown plan

The ERA is not designed to be spent down, so at the end of the day you can still leave more behind for your beneficiaries.

Why it matters

Three concerns, and one plan that covers all of them.

Each CPF LIFE plan is built to address a different concern.

Basic plan

Bequest

Leaves the most behind for your beneficiaries.

Standard plan

Income

Gives the highest starting payout.

Escalating plan

Inflation

Grows by 2% every year as prices rise.

No single plan can address all three.

That is where the ERA comes in. It is built in a way that addresses bequest, income and inflation at the same time.

What goes into it

An ERA is put together from three parts.

Annuity

A guaranteed income stream, so part of your income never depends on the market.

Market-linked plan

Regular premiums that grow with the market and pay an income when you need it.

Lump sum plan

A single premium invested for growth and income.

How much goes into each part depends on you, your budget and how much income you want. We work it out together with your own numbers.

When we meet

What to expect when we meet.

We go through a simple process together. By the end, you will see what CPF LIFE could pay you every month, and what your retirement income looks like against the life you want.

The process

Six steps, at your pace.

  1. 1

    We talk first

    Your family, your goals and the lifestyle you want in retirement. I listen before any numbers go on screen.

  2. 2

    We enter your CPF numbers

    Your date of birth, your OA, SA and MA balances, your salary and the age you plan to stop working.

  3. 3

    We project your CPF LIFE payout

    What your Retirement Account will look like at 55, and what CPF LIFE could pay you each month from 65 under the Basic, Standard and Escalating plans.

  4. 4

    We map your retirement income

    CPF LIFE laid against the monthly income you want, after inflation, so you can see any gap year by year.

  5. 5

    We design your ERA, if it helps

    If there is a gap, we look at how an ERA built on top of CPF LIFE can fill it, including income before 65 and what is left behind for your family.

  6. 6

    You take the plan home

    You get a clear report with your numbers. Then you decide, at your own pace.

In step 3 we compare all three CPF LIFE plans side by side.

What to bring

A few things make the session smoother.

  • Your latest CPF statement, or your CPF balances from the CPF app
  • A rough idea of the monthly income you would like in retirement
  • The age you plan to stop working
  • Any other income you expect, such as SRS or rental income
